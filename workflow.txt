Workflow (Tiered Processing)

1.LLM Keywords:
Keep this! The LLM helps find the best search words.

2.Embedding Sections:
Keep this too! Find the most relevant subsections using embeddings.

3.Tiered Processing:
Top Section (VIP Treatment):
Give the entire content of the top-ranked section to the LLM.
Other Sections (Helping Hand):
For the second and third-ranked sections:
Use embeddings to find the specific sentences or paragraphs related to the procedure.
Give only those extracted parts to the LLM.

4.LLM Extraction (Piece by Piece):
The LLM extracts procedure information, processing the top section as a whole and the other sections in extracted pieces.

5.Combine and Summarize:
The LLM combines all the extracted information into a flow property graph using JSON and creates a summary.

6.LLM Validation:
Keep this! The LLM checks its own work and correct if need


7.Store Result:
Keep the validated flow property graph JSON.
