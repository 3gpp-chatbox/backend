**Key Information and Description:**

The initial registration procedure is how a UE registers with the 5G network for the first time. The UE sends a `REGISTRATION REQUEST` message, and the network (AMF) responds with a `REGISTRATION ACCEPT` or `REGISTRATION REJECT` message.  The procedure involves several information elements related to UE capabilities, network slicing, security, and mobility management. There are different registration types such as intial, emergency, SNPN onboarding and disaster roaming, and the behaviour varies according to the specific registration type.
